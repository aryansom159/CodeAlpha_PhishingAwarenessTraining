What is Phishing?

Phishing is a cyber attack where attackers trick users into revealing passwords, bank details, or personal information using fake emails, messages, or websites.

Types of Phishing

  • Email Phishing
  • Smishing (SMS)
  • Vishing (Voice Calls)
  • Spear Phishing

How to Identify Phishing?

  • Unknown sender
  • Urgent message
  • Grammar mistakes
  • Suspicious links
  • Unexpected attachments

Fake Email Example

From: support@paytm-security.xyz

Your account will be blocked within 24 hours. Click the link below to verify your account immediately.

❌ This is a phishing email.

Safety Tips

  • Never click unknown links.
  • Check website URL carefully.
  • Use strong passwords.
  • Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA).
  • Keep your software updated.
